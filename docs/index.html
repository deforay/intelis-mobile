InteLIS Mobile

A free and open-source Android app for registering laboratory test requests and viewing results in the InteLIS lab information system.

InteLIS Mobile lets staff at health facilities and laboratories register HIV Viral Load, Early Infant Diagnosis and COVID-19 test requests on a phone or tablet, record results, and look up results once the laboratory has released them. Requests are stored on the device so they can be entered without a network connection and synchronised later.

The app is a client. It connects to the InteLIS server operated by your own ministry of health, laboratory network or organisation, using a server address you enter when you sign in.
